# Appendix H: The 21 DASS Questions

The Depression, Anxiety, and Stress Scale (DASS-21) is a 21-item self-report questionnaire designed to measure the severity of the core symptoms of depression, anxiety, and stress.

## Response Scale
Participants are asked to use 4-point severity/frequency scales to rate the extent to which they have experienced each state over the past week:
* **0** - Did not apply to me at all
* **1** - Applied to me to some degree, or some of the time
* **2** - Applied to me to a considerable degree, or a good part of time
* **3** - Applied to me very much, or most of the time

## DASS-21 Questionnaire Items
1. I found it hard to wind down. (S)
2. I was aware of dryness of my mouth. (A)
3. I couldn't seem to experience any positive feeling at all. (D)
4. I experienced breathing difficulty (e.g. excessively rapid breathing, breathlessness in the absence of physical exertion). (A)
5. I found it difficult to work up the initiative to do things. (D)
6. I tended to over-react to situations. (S)
7. I experienced trembling (e.g. in the hands). (A)
8. I felt that I was using a lot of nervous energy. (S)
9. I was worried about situations in which I might panic and make a fool of myself. (A)
10. I felt that I had nothing to look forward to. (D)
11. I found myself getting agitated. (S)
12. I found it difficult to relax. (S)
13. I felt down-hearted and blue. (D)
14. I was intolerant of anything that kept me from getting on with what I was doing. (S)
15. I felt I was close to panic. (A)
16. I was unable to become enthusiastic about anything. (D)
17. I felt I wasn't worth much as a person. (D)
18. I felt that I was rather touchy. (S)
19. I was aware of the action of my heart in the absence of physical exertion (e.g. sense of heart rate increase, heart missing a beat). (A)
20. I felt scared without any good reason. (A)
21. I felt that life was meaningless. (D)

## Subscale Mapping
* **Depression (D):** Items 3, 5, 10, 13, 16, 17, 21
* **Anxiety (A):** Items 2, 4, 7, 9, 15, 19, 20
* **Stress (S):** Items 1, 6, 8, 11, 12, 14, 18

## Score Calculation
Scores for depression, anxiety, and stress are calculated by summing the scores for the relevant items. To match the scoring of the full 42-item DASS, the sum for each subscale must be **multiplied by 2**. 

## Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer
*This questionnaire and the resulting scores are not intended to be used as a medical diagnostic tool. High scores on any of the scales indicate a presence of symptoms that may warrant further assessment by a qualified psychological or medical professional.*

# Appendix J: API Endpoints and Deployment Information

## PrePal ML Backend API Endpoints

The PrePal system utilizes a Python Flask-based backend server to handle Machine Learning predictions and AI chat processing. 

### 1. Prediction Endpoint
* **Endpoint:** `/api/predict`
* **Method:** `POST`
* **Description:** Accepts student academic and psychological data, processes warnings/acknowledgements, and returns a predicted future GPA based on the selected ML model type (Pre-Sem, Mid-Sem, Master).
* **Request Payload (JSON):**
  ```json
  {
    "student_id": "ST12345",
    "student_type": 1, 
    "attendance": 85.0,
    "study_hours": 12.0,
    "sleep_hours": 8.0,
    "stress_level": 2.5,
    "cgpa": 3.2,
    "depression_score": 10,
    "anxiety_score": 8,
    "stress_score": 12,
    "results": [
      {
        "moduleName": "Data Structures",
        "credits": 4,
        "mid_mark": 12.0,
        "assignment_mark": 15.0
      }
    ]
  }
  ```
* **Response (JSON):** Returns the `predicted_future_gpa`, `semester_gpa`, a `motivation_tip`, `eligible` status (for finals), and an array of `acknowledgements_required` based on health and academic rules.

### 2. AI Chatbot Endpoint
* **Endpoint:** `/api/ai_chat`
* **Method:** `POST`
* **Description:** Processes conversational queries from the student, utilizing their academic context to provide personalized responses.
* **Request Payload (JSON):** Requires `user_message` and `student_context`.
* **Response (JSON):** Returns the AI's `reply` and processing `status`.

## Deployment Information
* **Hosting Platform:** The ML server is designed for deployment on cloud platforms (e.g., Heroku, Render) using a standard WSGI server.
* **Server Configuration:** The application uses `gunicorn` as the HTTP server, as defined in the deployment `Procfile` (`web: gunicorn app:app`).
* **Environment:** Python 3.x, relying on libraries such as Flask, scikit-learn (for `.pkl` models), and associated AI dependencies listed in `requirements.txt`.
